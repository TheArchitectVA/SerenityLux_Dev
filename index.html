WHY SINGLE-DONOR SLAVIC HAIR OUTLASTS COMMERCIAL EXTENSIONS BY YEARS

98% of the global hair market is comprised of "floor collection" factory hair—strands swept from multiple sources, bathed in harsh hydrochloric acids to strip the cuticle, and shellacked with industrial silicone that washes away after 3 shampoos.
