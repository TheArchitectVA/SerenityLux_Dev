How It Works

SERENITY LUX ARCHIVE

Ten Ponytails Dropped Every Wednesday at 12 PM EST

1. Limited Weekly Collector Capsules

We release in limited collector capsules. Exactly ten ponytails are dropped every Wednesday at 12 PM EST. Each pony is one of one and never restocked. Once it is claimed, the donor strand profile enters our permanent archive.

2. Whole Ponytail & Studio Archive

You purchase the entire raw ponytail, which becomes a working archive for your salon or personal install. One ponytail typically yields two to three installs, depending on your intended length, density, and finish.

3. In-Person Matching & Real-Time Installs

Stock a curated range of textures and tones so you can consult and install in a single appointment. Invite clients to feel the unmatched cuticle purity, inspect the root alignment, and build custom dimensional blends without chemical compromises.

4. Hand-Bonded in Haus

Select based on your install preference: pre-bonded Keratin K-Tips, individual bond strips, or raw bulk hair. Every single tip and weft is precision-bonded by hand in our Serenity Lux haus.
